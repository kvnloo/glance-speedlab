# E012 result — Sub-128 image-token budget

## Interpretation

Mixed speed result; guardrail rejected. On 4B, budget 48 reduced p50 from 793.8 to 727.2 ms (1.092×) and retained every fixed decision, but maximum probability drift was 0.130 against the preregistered 0.10 limit. Budget 32 reached 1.126× but changed one decision. On 2B, 32/48 reached 1.158×/1.121× but both changed one fixed decision. Budget 64 failed on all seven tall-receipt attempts because processor rounding emitted 77 tokens above the configured cap. Budgets 96 and 128 were identical. Keep 128 as the default; 4B/48 merits a larger labeled frontier study, not adoption.
